How to play

Move all 52 cards to the four foundations, building each one up by suit from ace to king. In the tableau, stack cards downwards in alternating colours; only a king can fill an empty column. When you're stuck, turn cards over from the stock.

Mouse and touch

  • Drag a card, or a run of cards, onto another pile.
  • Double-click or double-tap a card to send it to a foundation, or, with Auto-move on, to a column that takes it.
  • Click the stock to draw. Click the empty stock to turn the waste over.
  • Autocollect sends every card that can go home to the foundations.

Keyboard

  • ← ↑ → ↓ Move between cards and piles
  • Enter / Space Pick up the focused card, then drop it on the focused pile. On the stock, draw.
  • Esc Put the card back, or close this panel
  • Z Undo. Each undo costs 5 moves.
